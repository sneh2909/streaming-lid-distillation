## Interview questions

1. **Why must the final `L` logits be withheld?** Each uses `L` future feature frames. Before those frames arrive, whole-pass padding makes the logits provisional; publishing them violates the bounded-lookahead contract.
2. **What is the difference between semantic switch onset and an online persistent decision?** Onset is the first anchor of a run identified in hindsight. An online three-anchor decision exists only when the third anchor, including its right context, is available.
3. **Does the bake-off prove ECAPA is the best distillation teacher?** No. It supports retaining ECAPA in this pilot on restricted accuracy and cost. It lacks real calls, calibration/distillation outcomes, multiple natural switches, and confidence intervals.
4. **Which target-ablation arm is deployable at the tested latency?** Only `prefix_delta250`; full-utterance and centered-two-second targets expose more future context and are diagnostic upper/comparison arms.
5. **What must a resumable experiment identity contain?** Exact inputs, driver and imported-code hashes, settings, dependency versions, model revisions and weight hashes, target-generation identity, and a per-arm schema/analysis version.
