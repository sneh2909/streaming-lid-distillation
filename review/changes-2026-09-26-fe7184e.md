## Findings

### [METHOD] scripts/teacher_targets.py:143-155,199-228 and experiments/target-type-ablation/run.py:291-294,369-386,404-435 — Linear interpolation exceeds the stated future-evidence budget

Both generators run the teacher every 25 feature frames (250 ms) and linearly interpolate those anchor posteriors onto the 10 ms grid. An interpolated target immediately after anchor `a` therefore consults anchor `a+25`. For `prefix_delta250` and the main local-window target, that later anchor itself consumes audio through its frame end plus 250 ms, so the earlier target can depend on audio about **490 ms** beyond its own frame end. Only exact anchor frames have the advertised 250 ms horizon; 24 of each 25 ordinary frames exceed it. The same effect raises the centred arm's maximum future horizon from the stated 1,000 ms to about **1,240 ms**. The delayed student prediction aligned to target `i` has evidence only through `i+D+L = i+25` frames, so these targets leak future evidence unavailable to that prediction.

Use a causal previous-anchor hold, run the teacher at every frame, or reserve the anchor hop inside the future-context budget. Record and validate the effective per-frame availability. Until this is corrected and rerun, `prefix_delta250` is not evidence for a deployable context-matched target; the main switch-target path has the same qualification.

## Interview questions

1. **Why must the final `L` logits be withheld?** Each uses `L` future feature frames. Before those frames arrive, whole-pass padding makes the logits provisional; publishing them violates the bounded-lookahead contract.
2. **What is the difference between semantic switch onset and an online persistent decision?** Onset is the first anchor of a run identified in hindsight. An online three-anchor decision exists only when the third anchor, including its right context, is available.
3. **Does the bake-off prove ECAPA is the best distillation teacher?** No. It supports retaining ECAPA in this pilot on restricted accuracy and cost. It lacks real calls, calibration/distillation outcomes, multiple natural switches, and confidence intervals.
4. **Which target-ablation arm is proven deployable at the tested latency?** None as implemented. `prefix_delta250` is causal at its anchor points, but linear interpolation makes most 10 ms targets depend on a later anchor and exceed the 250 ms evidence budget.
5. **What must a resumable experiment identity contain?** Exact inputs, driver and imported-code hashes, settings, dependency versions, model revisions and weight hashes, target-generation identity, and a per-arm schema/analysis version.
